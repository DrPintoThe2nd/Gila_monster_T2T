#### Gila Monster Recombination Map

gilamonster_AVE_2MbWindow_50kbStep_G20MU.csv

	This version of the map has been averaged in 2Mb windows with a 50kb step. 
	We've used this approach before to look at longer term recombination rates in a species
	(see Foley et al 2026 - https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41225009/)

gilamonster_perchromosome.pdf gilamonster_chrZ.pdf

	These are overview plots of the whole genome as well as the Z.

gilamonster_rptmsk_F4.PREDICT.BSCORRECTED.txt

	This is the raw output from Relernn. Each of the recombination rate estimates have
	a 95% confidence interval.
